QUALITY BEFORE SCALE

The benchmark is allowed to say “not yet.”

On the 30-resume sample, human reviewers agreed with each other on vacancy order only 56.3% of the time. Laya’s average pairwise agreement with their mean ranks was 53.2%; the simple BM25 baseline reached 58.2%.

These labels test a different task from ranking many candidates for one job. That gap is documented instead of being hidden behind a match percentage.
